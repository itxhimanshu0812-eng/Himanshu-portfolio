About me

I'm a B.Sc. student studying Zoology and Botany, with Computer Science as a minor subject. Alongside my degree, I completed an ADCA (Advanced Diploma in Computer Applications), which gave me a solid base in everyday computer use.

Computers have become the part of my studies I enjoy the most. I use Microsoft Office regularly, I understand the basics of Tally, and I'm now learning HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Python so I can create real websites and small programs.
